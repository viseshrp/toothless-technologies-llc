# Company operating guide

This guide opens Toothless Technologies LLC, qualifies an internal opportunity,
and routes work through the executive team.

## 1. Begin or resume operations

Read `OPERATIONS.md` first. When an active engagement is named there, also read
its `PROJECT_TRACKER.md` and only the linked active or blocked cards. Continue
the recorded next action instead of reconstructing state from chat history.

Open the repository in Codex and send:

```text
Act as the CEO of Toothless Technologies LLC. Read AGENTS.md and the company and
playbook files. Read OPERATIONS.md and resume its recorded next action. If no
engagement is active, direct Sales to identify three current target-account
opportunities from public market signals. Have Revenue, Finance, Technology,
Operations, and Legal qualify them. Select the strongest opportunity, create
its isolated workspace from clients/_template, staff the smallest accountable
team, and brief me as the CPO when a product decision is required. Route every
non-product assignment to the correct company role. Create or update the owning
functional project card before work begins and update every affected project
before pausing. Do not perform outreach or imply that the target organization
is a client.
```

No external product account is required to begin. Repository artifacts remain
authoritative.

The COO owns the company checkpoint and project hygiene audit. Product
Operations owns board administration for Product Management, Research, and
Design & Accessibility. The CPO is not responsible for card maintenance.

## 2. Review the CEO brief

The CEO brief must state:

- the target account and supported public market signal;
- which statements are sourced facts, inferences, internal assumptions, or
  unknowns;
- strategic fit, revenue logic, delivery feasibility, capacity, and risk;
- why the CEO accepted this opportunity over the alternatives;
- the active team and each member's assignment; and
- the product decision now owned by the CPO.

If the brief suggests a real request, relationship, or affiliation, the Legal
and Risk Advisor must correct it before the engagement proceeds.

## 3. Run the product function

The CPO answers in her own words and asks for any evidence needed. Product
Operations records approved choices in `DECISION_LOG.md` without changing their
meaning.

The CPO owns:

1. target user, problem, and desired outcome;
2. product strategy, principles, value proposition, and non-goals;
3. discovery questions and interpretation of product evidence;
4. priority, MVP scope, roadmap, metrics, and product tradeoffs; and
5. experience direction, requirements, acceptance criteria, and product
   readiness.

Specialists must bring evidence, alternatives, a recommendation, and explicit
tradeoffs. They should not quiz the CPO or add personal-performance commentary.

## 4. Route non-product work

The CPO must not be assigned:

- application code, tests, infrastructure, or technical configuration;
- target-account outreach, sales administration, or contract activity;
- financial models, legal language, staffing logistics, or status collation;
- delivery administration, issue routing, or agent scheduling; or
- defect repair, security remediation, or deployment operations.

The CEO routes each assignment to the accountable executive or specialist and
returns to the CPO only when product authority is required.

## 5. Use the Board product advisor selectively

The Independent Board Director for Product and Strategy is available for major
product and portfolio matters. The CPO can request an opinion with:

```text
CEO, ask the Independent Board Director for Product and Strategy to review this
decision. The director should examine the written evidence, challenge the
assumptions, identify second-order consequences, and issue an advisory memo.
The director must not write my decision or manage the product team.
```

The advisor is not part of routine engagement staffing.

## 6. Close the engagement

At closeout, use:

```text
CEO, run the engagement closeout. Confirm product and technical acceptance,
have Revenue and Operations prepare internal market-readiness materials, record
financial and delivery implications, identify any company-level portfolio or
governance issue, and release the engagement team. Nothing should be published
or represented as client work.
```

The final record belongs in `ENGAGEMENT_REVIEW.md`. A Board memorandum is added
only when a governance trigger exists.

## Correcting role drift

Use one of these direct corrections:

```text
This is a product decision. Return the evidence, alternatives, recommendation,
and tradeoffs to me as the CPO.
```

```text
This is not product work. Route it to the accountable company role and return
when product authority is required.
```

```text
This is routine product work. Remove the Board advisor and continue through the
normal management chain.
```
