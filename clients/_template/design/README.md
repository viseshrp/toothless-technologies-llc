# Design workspace

Store or reference product-design work here.

Recommended artifacts:

- `JOURNEY.md` — current journey, pain points, and evidence;
- `CONCEPTS.md` — at least three solution directions and tradeoffs;
- `USER_FLOW.md` or Mermaid diagram — approved flow;
- exported wireframes or prototype images;
- `USABILITY_PLAN.md` — hypothesis, tasks, participants/proxies, thresholds;
- `USABILITY_FINDINGS.md` — labeled synthetic/proxy findings and limitations;
- `DESIGN_REVIEW.md` — CPO decision, accessibility review, and open questions.

For a Figma or Penpot artifact, record the link only if it is safe to make
public. Export a viewable artifact and summarize the design rationale so the
engagement remains understandable without external access.

The Product Designer creates options. The human CPO approves experience
direction and product tradeoffs.
