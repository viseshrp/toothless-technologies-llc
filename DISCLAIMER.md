# Independent-work and non-affiliation disclosure

Greyshore Product Partners operates this repository as an internal product
consulting environment. All current account work is speculative and based on
public market signals. No named organization is a client.

An opportunity, request, budget, interview, participant, dataset, roadmap,
design, prototype, launch plan, or business result in an engagement is an
internal assumption or synthetic artifact unless a cited public source supports
the individual fact.

Using a real organization name does not mean that the organization requested,
reviewed, approved, sponsored, or is affiliated with the work. Organization
names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Agents must not contact people or organizations, represent Greyshore as their
vendor, submit proposals, purchase services, accept legal terms, create paid
accounts, or deploy a public product. Public sources must be cited. Synthetic
evidence must be labeled. The repository must not contain confidential or
personal data.

Every new engagement must repeat this statement in its `README.md` and
`BRIEF.md`:

> **Independent speculative work. The named organization is not a client of
> Greyshore Product Partners and did not request, review, or endorse this work.**
